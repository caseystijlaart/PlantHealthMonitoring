= Human Values
Strengthened values include environmental awareness, autonomy in decision-making, and learning about AI and plants.
Placing humans at the center of AI development — where the system serves and educates rather than replaces human judgment — is a core principle of responsible AI (Hu et al., 2025).
Ensuring that users retain meaningful control over how they interpret and act on AI suggestions is equally important for preserving human autonomy (Väänänen et al., 2021).

**Improvements:** Emphasize educational framing, provide autonomy in decision-making, and avoid gamification that pressures compliance.
