= Transparency
The system communicates plant recommendations as suggestions, not authoritative commands.
Limitations and operational principles are clearly documented in the dashboard, reflecting the principle that users should be able to understand and question AI-generated outputs (Gunning & Aha, 2019).

[quote, Gunning & Aha, DARPA's Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) Program (2019)]
____
AI systems whose learned models and decisions can be understood and appropriately trusted by end users.
____

Transparency in AI systems is increasingly recognized as a foundational requirement for building user trust and ensuring responsible deployment (Wirtz et al., 2025).

**Improvements:** Add FAQs, visual explanations of AI reasoning, and feedback channels for users to challenge outputs.
