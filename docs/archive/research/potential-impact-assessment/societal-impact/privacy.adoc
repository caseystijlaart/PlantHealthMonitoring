= Privacy
The system processes environmental data locally, no personal identifiers are collected.
In case of dashboard integration requiring external access, anonymization and encryption are enforced in line with the principles of the General Data Protection Regulation (European Parliament & Council, 2016).

**Potential risks:** Indirect profiling if users’ patterns are combined with other datasets, a concern relevant even for seemingly non-personal sensor data (Radanliev et al., 2024).

**Improvements:** Introduce strict data retention policies, anonymization safeguards, and user control over any transmitted data.
