= IoT-Based Plant Monitoring Systems

Currently, a variety of plant monitoring systems are available, most of which have been developed by students, hobbyists, or small-scale developers.
These systems generally focus on environmental monitoring rather than advanced AI-based analysis.


Many of these solutions are open-source, providing access to hardware and software designs, while others are proprietary.
The majority of existing systems do not integrate AI or Machine Learning; instead, they monitor environmental parameters, such as soil moisture, temperature, and humidity, and notify users when predefined thresholds are exceeded.


Some implementations are more advanced, incorporating automated irrigation systems that water plants when certain conditions are met, improving plant care efficiency.
Other systems are simpler, only sending alerts or notifications to the user without any automation.
 

**Examples of existing systems include:**  

* Plant Monitoring System by Shishir3D
* IoT-based Plant Health Care System (MDPI)
* IoT-Based Plant Health Care System (Academia.edu)
* IoT-Based Smart Irrigation System (Academia.edu)
