= Commercial Solutions
Several commercial solutions exist for crop and plant monitoring; however, most of these systems are designed for large-scale agricultural applications rather than small-scale or individual plant care. These solutions often integrate advanced sensors and IoT platforms to monitor soil conditions, plant health, and environmental factors at scale. 

While they provide reliable data and automated management for farms and commercial crops, their focus and cost make them less suitable for typical household or small-scale plant monitoring projects. Nonetheless, these commercial systems demonstrate the potential of IoT and sensor integration for predictive and automated plant care.

**Examples of commercial systems include:**  

* Connected Green – Soil Moisture Sensor: A commercial sensor solution for monitoring soil moisture in large crop environments.  
  Available at: https://connectedgreen.nl/bodemvochtsensor

* Cropwise Interra Scan: A precision agriculture platform that uses IoT devices and analytics for crop monitoring and management.  
  Available at: https://www.cropwise.com/netherlands/interra-scan

* Vivent Biosignals – Food and Agriculture Inputs: Provides sensor-based monitoring and analytics for plant and crop health on a commercial scale.  
  Available at: https://vivent-biosignals.com/solutions/food-and-ag-inputs

These systems illustrate how IoT and sensor technologies are applied in commercial agriculture, but also highlight the gap for small-scale, AI/ML-based plant care systems suitable for personal or hobbyist use.
